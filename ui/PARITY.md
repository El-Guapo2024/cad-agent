# FreeCAD parity, command by command

Compared against FreeCAD main at 3160daf1e2b6 (2026-09-29). The command lists come
straight from `src/Gui/Workbench.cpp`: `StdWorkbench::setupMenuBar`, `setupToolBars`
and `setupContextMenu`. The shortcuts come from each command's `sAccel` in
`src/Gui/Command*.cpp`, and the behaviour from the files named per section.

Every row marked done was checked in the page, by the tests run on 2026-09-29 and 2026-09-30.

Legend: **done**, **partial** (says what differs), **missing**, **n/a** (and why).

Why most n/a rows are n/a: the UI shows parts built from Python. There is no FreeCAD document
and no save. Edits are `cad set` (a part's PARAMS), `cad place` (a rigid move in placements.toml)
and, for a part made of a PartDesign feature list (cad_agent/feature.py), `cad feature` (its Body's
sketches, pads and pockets: see "PartDesign", below). Creating or deleting a hand-written part is the
agent's job, in the code.

**Standard views**: all done, including Align to selection (`View3DInventorViewer::alignToSelection`, below).
- Axonometric: Isometric (0), Dimetric, Trimetric
- Fit all (V,F) and Fit selection (V,S). Fit selection frames the whole of each selected part, whatever faces, edges or vertices of it are selected, which is what the real FreeCAD 26.3 does (measured with `scripts/fcbehave.py --only fit` and `scripts/oursselect.mjs`, 2026-10-21): `ViewProvider::getBoundingBox(subname)` returns the part's whole box for a Face (`SoBrepFaceSet::getBoundingBox` is the inherited one) and, measured, for an Edge and a Vertex as well; for a part that has a Placement it returns that box without the Placement, so the camera lands away from the part (not copied). FREECAD_AUDIT.md 5.2 read the source as if elements were fitted; they are not. A perspective camera stands R / tan(heightAngle / 2) from the centre, 1 / min(1, aspect) times that in a tall view (FreeCAD: 232.893 for R 96.468 at 45°; the audit's "8.2 % too far" was R / sin), and Fit selection leaves the heightAngle where it is while Fit all sets 45° (`viewAll`)
- Align to selection (`alignToSelection`, measured over 264 cases from 12 starting views; `scripts/oursselect.mjs` against `fcbehave.py --only align`): the camera turns about the focal point and the view is neither moved nor zoomed, to look straight at the selection from the side it is already on (the direction is flipped when the camera's z is nearer the opposite one), rolled to the quarter turn nearest its own roll from the direction of the face's longest straight edge. A planar face gives its normal, a straight edge its direction, a planar curve the axis of its plane; a part's several faces give the sum of their normals; the parts' sums add up; with no straight edge the screen's x is `Rotation(-Z, direction)`'s. Enabled once anything is selected (FreeCAD's isActive is a 3D view; it does nothing for a selection with no direction). A face that is not planar (the side of a cylinder, a sphere, a fillet) gives the normal at the middle of its (u, v) bounds and the direction of its longest straight edge (the "Single non-planar face" branch of `Part::Feature::getCameraAlignmentDirection`): the mesh does not have them, so `scene.py` works them out from the shape (`curved_faces`, with `BRepGProp_Face` as FreeCAD does) and scene.json carries them per body (`curved`), turned with the body when a pose or a drag moves it; a cylinder's side seen from the Front view, exactly across it, is turned to the side the last bits of the normal (1.2e-16 at u = pi) give, as in FreeCAD, so the normal is not rounded. A camera exactly across the face (a Front view and a top face) has its side decided by rounding, in FreeCAD by Coin's float32 quaternion (the Front view's z axis is 6e-8 above the horizontal, so a top face is met from above and a bottom face is turned to be seen from above too); the same float32 arithmetic is used here, so the choice is the same. 264 of 264 cases agree with FreeCAD 26.3 (measured on Linux, 2026-10-23)

## Mouse and navigation cube

- **Corner coordinate system** (View3DInventorViewer::drawAxisCross): done. FreeCAD's arrows (`createAxisArrowGeometry`), AxisX/Y/ZColor (#CC3333, #33CC33, #3333CC), its X, Y, Z letter bitmaps (`CornerCrossLetters.h`), a 45° perspective mini view at the bottom right, 10 % of the shorter side (CornerCoordSystemSize, 2–100), sorted by depth. three-cad-viewer's own axes marker (bottom left) isn't drawn.

- **Navigation**: done. `src/nav.ts` ports `src/Gui/Navigation/`:
  - `NavigationStyle`: pan on the focal plane, zoom as e^step, zoom at the cursor, roll, `lookAtPoint` (a quick middle click centres the view on the point under it), and the rotation-centre marker.
  - The orbit, through `FCSphereSheetProjector` over Coin's `SbSphereSheetProjector`: Rounded Arcball (the default), Trackball, Trackball Classic, Free Turntable and Turntable. Rotation about the window centre, the cursor, or the object centre.
  - All twelve styles, event by event: Blender, CAD (the default), Gesture, MayaGesture, OpenCascade, OpenInventor, OpenSCAD, Revit, Siemens NX, SolidWorks, TinkerCAD and Touchpad.
  - The wheel: 120 a notch, ZoomStep 0.2, InvertZoom. A touchpad pans (Shift orbits), as `wheelAction` does on a Mac. PageUp and PageDown zoom at the cursor.
  - Gestures (`processPinchEvent`, `pinchAction`, `SoTouchEvents.cpp`). A trackpad pinch (Chrome and Firefox send it as Ctrl+wheel) and Safari's pinch and twist work as Qt's native gestures: they zoom and turn at the cursor, and DisableTouchTilt never blocks the turn. On a touchscreen the first finger is the left button. Two fingers make a QPinchGesture: their centre pans, their spread zooms, and their turn rolls the view when DisableTouchTilt is off (it's on by default). The Gesture style enters GestureState (panning), except on a Mac, where FreeCAD hands gestures straight to NavigationStyle. MayaGesture drags with its own pinch, which ignores the tilt setting. OpenInventor drops gestures outside selection, as FreeCAD does. A pinch never ends in a click.
  - `processClickEvent`: a left press within the double-click time of the last one waits for its release (issue #0002433). A drag box clears that state. The double-click time is `QApplication::doubleClickInterval()`, the system's: 500 ms by default on a Mac and on Windows (measured on this Mac), 400 on X11, not a fixed 400. ## The formula engine, 2026-10-23

`src/expr.ts` (with `expr-grammar.ts`, the LALR tables of App/Expression.y written by `scripts/exprgen.py`, and `expr-py.ts`, the Python values and operators FreeCAD's expressions compute with) is a port of App/Expression.cpp, Expression.l/.y, ObjectIdentifier.cpp and Base/Quantity: results are Python values (int, float, bool, str, None, Quantity, list, tuple, Vector), errors are the exception classes and messages FreeCAD raises. `scripts/exprdiff.py` holds it to the real FreeCAD 26.3 (3160daf1e2b6) on 75,558 expressions: 0 differ, strict (types, values, units, exception classes and messages). Three new random sets (EXPRDIFF_SEED 0 to 2, about 75,600 cases each, recorded on 2026-10-23 with the Linux build of the same commit) differ only in C library message texts (glibc's "Numerical result out of range" and "-nan" against macOS's "Result too large" and "nan"). `tests/test_expr_parity.py` runs 1,974 of the stored answers on CI. The Expression Editor shows a result that is not a number as the text it becomes, as checkExpression does, with OK off. Deliberately different:
- No Matrix, Placement or Rotation: functions that make one raise NotImplementedError (251 cases, set aside by the comparison). Nothing here holds one: a field is a length, an angle or a number.
- FreeCAD's essentiallyInteger casts an integral double above INT_MAX to a C int, so `3000000000`, `1e10` and `2^31` come out as -2147483648 (and `-2147483648` as 2147483648). Ours gives the number (`QUIRKS.int32Cast`, on only in the comparison; with it off, those 282 cases are the only differences, and they agree with Python).
- A string, list or tuple longer than 10 million items, or a % field wider than that, raises MemoryError ("result too large to compute here") where Python would build it, and an integer power over 4 million bits likewise; nesting deeper than the JavaScript stack raises RecursionError. A browser tab would freeze or crash first.
- The object and document an expression may name are the part the field belongs to; a part's int parameter is an int (PropertyInteger), a length or angle a Quantity, any other number a float (PropertyFloat). Other objects and documents are not found, as there is no FreeCAD document here.
