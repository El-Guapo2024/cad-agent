# mini_conveyor — mechanical spec

## Brief

A bench-top conveyor module for the v1 mini PCB line (UV laser -> LumenPnP -> oven): fixed rails for one standard 170 x 170 carrier holding a 150 x 150 board, the carrier's top at the LumenPnP's board height (137 mm over the bench), flat belts on a hidden return, one stepper driving both belts through a shaft. Features meshed from NeoDen's J08 and SuperHouse's PCBCONVEYOR (see ../pcb_conveyor/research/REFERENCES.md).
