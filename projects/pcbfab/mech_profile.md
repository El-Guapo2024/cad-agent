# pcbfab — mechanical spec

## Brief

Desktop PCB fab, one machine: LumenPnP v4.2.0 with its Y axis 300 mm longer (lumenpnp_long), a hot plate under the head for reflow and via cure, and a laser zone behind it (20 W MOPA fiber source, SG7110 galvo, F160 lens on a 2040 bridge) that drills, etches, opens solder mask and marks the legend. The head carries the board between the two.
