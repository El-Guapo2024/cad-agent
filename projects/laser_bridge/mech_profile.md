# laser_bridge — mechanical spec

## Brief

The fab's UV laser on a bridge that is its own focus axis: JPT Lark 355 nm, SG7110 galvo and Jenoptik telecentric lens on a plate under two 4040 beams, the beams riding up and down 4040 posts on MGN15 rails, a Tr8x2 screw and NEMA 17 at each post.
