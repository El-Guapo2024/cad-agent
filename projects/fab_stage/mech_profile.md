# fab_stage — mechanical spec

## Brief

The PCB fab's one fixture: the board in a flip frame over a heated vacuum platen that drops away for the flip, on an XY ball-screw stage that stitches the laser's 100 mm field over a 229 x 305 mm board and carries it under the pick-and-place head.
