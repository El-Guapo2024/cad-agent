# head_tools — mechanical spec

## Brief

The process tools on LumenPnP's head: two ViscoTec eco-PEN300 dispensers (solder paste, via paste), a Nordson PICO Pulse jet (UV solder mask and legend ink) and a Panasonic HG-C1030 laser height sensor, on an 8 mm lift beside the head's nozzles.
