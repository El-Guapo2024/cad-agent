# Credits

This conveyor's layout follows **PCBCONVEYOR** by Jonathan Oxer, SuperHouse Automation Pty Ltd
(https://github.com/SuperHouse/PCBCONVEYOR, commit aa8a56e), licensed under the TAPR Open Hardware License v1.0:
an edge-belt PCB conveyor with an adjustable width for linking pick-and-place, reflow and inspection machines.
None of its files are copied here; the parts are redrawn for machined aluminium and extrusion, and its sizes were
measured from its 3MF parts only to understand the layout. This derived design is offered under the same license.

Interface numbers come from the SMEMA Mechanical Equipment Interface Standard 1.2, as included in that repository
(Resources/smema1.2.pdf).
